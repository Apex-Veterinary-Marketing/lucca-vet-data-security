<p id="">Hello Clint,</p><p id="">Just this last week we were in a meeting with a new hospital and the practice manager had asked me how long have I been in veterinary medicine. It’s crazy to think it’s been almost 7 years! If you would have told me back in 2014 when I was graduating from law school that I’d be running a technology company helping veterinary hospitals. I would have told you, “You’re crazy!”</p><p id="">But I wouldn’t change it for the world. You all have had such an impact on my life that I have zero regrets. So thank you and thank you for taking the time to read this email.</p><p id="">So this had me thinking…</p><p id="">How many of you have been told by a cloud-based Practice Management System that <strong id="">“Once you go to the cloud, you can turn off your server”.</strong> Then come to find out years later that you can’t just turn off the server.</p><p id="">And it gets even more complicated…..</p><p id="">You have your IT company telling you<strong id=""> “We have to keep the server for critical IT functions”</strong>. Just in the last month, we have spoken with two separate hospitals that are paying on average $1000.00 per month for a cloud server that they don’t need!</p><h3 id="">So what is it!? Can we be serverless or do we really need a server to function?</h3><p id="">The good news is! You don’t need a server once you go cloud-based, we can get rid of it, and here’s why your IT person thinks you need one. I.e. the jobs your server is still performing.</p><p id=""><strong id="">1) DHCP</strong> – This is how your computers get an address. Once you go cloud-based you really need a Firewall protecting your network. The best part is that your firewall can also perform DHCP. Thus we move this job to the firewall and away from the server</p><p id=""><strong id="">2) Domain</strong> – A domain helps to control logins and printers. But in most hospital settings we all share a “Tech” or “Clinic” login. These logins can be moved to the local computer in what we call a “workgroup”. And most cloud practice management systems have cloud print tools to help map your printers. Thus allowing us to remove these jobs from the server.</p><p id=""><strong id="">3) File Sharing</strong> – There are a couple of ways you can handle this so let’s break it into two options. Allowing us to get rid of this job for the server.</p><p id="">a) Move your files to your tech station or lab PC which we can then share and backup for approx $20 per month.<br>b) Use a cloud file-sharing solution like Office 365 or Citrix Sharefile.</p><p id=""><strong id="">4) Reminder Services etc</strong> – You may have a reminder service or coupon service like Greenline that’s running on your server as well. The good news is that these services or apps don’t require a lot of horsepower to run. Thus we move them to the Tech station PC that we’re backing up and BOOM! This job has also been moved off the server.</p><p id="">That’s it! Those are the 4 primary jobs your server is doing once you are using a cloud-based practice management system. While most IT companies are afraid to embrace the serverless environment as they are afraid it will cost them their job. I mean who wants to lose $12000.00 a year in revenue hosting servers?</p><p id="">We do! It’s the future and we stand by our motto “Everything in IT you need and nothing you don’t!” Why spend money on IT that you don’t have to!?</p><p id="">I hope you found that inciteful and had an amazing holiday weekend! My tomorrow bring you lots of puppy appointments!</p>
