Meet

Clint Latham, J.D.

Director of Veterinary Data Security

Lucca, founded by Clint Latham, now manages technology for hundreds of veterinary hospitals across North America. With a deep understanding of the veterinary industry’s unique challenges, Clint has emerged as an international speaker on technology in veterinary medicine, authored over 150 articles on cybersecurity and IT in veterinary hospitals, and developed CE accredited courses for Patterson Veterinary University. Clint’s mission is to empower veterinary professionals with the knowledge to protect their data and streamline their technology solutions, focusing on delivering quality care for our four-legged family members. This dedication stems from his personal experience as a pet owner of two senior Yorkies, emphasizing the importance of reliable veterinary care. Through Lucca, Clint offers a balanced approach to IT services, ensuring veterinary hospitals are equipped with what they need, safeguarding against cyber threats while managing IT costs efficiently.

Meet

Logan Kroll-Gunter

Director of Customer Success

Mom to a 14-year-old miniature dachshund, Logan was no stranger to the vet but when she left a clinic with a 5-month-old American shepherd on her 2nd day working in the veterinary industry, she knew she had found her passion.

Logan enjoys working with practice owners and their teams to understand their IT difficulties and come up with solutions that work for each unique practice. She believes IT should not be complicated and wants to help educate practices to ensure they are making smart decisions when it comes to technology.

Meet

Keelan Bell

Veterinary Technology Specialist

Keelan has lived an extraordinary life. Born in South Africa and whisked into a lifestyle of adventure since the age of twelve, he has experienced many different cultures and ways of living while traveling with his family in an old Land Rover. From the rarefied air of the high Andes, to the Alaskan glaciers and the dense swamps of the Congo, Keelan has learned countless lessons, but one that rings true is that no matter where you go in the world, a pet’s love never changes, animals possess dignity, courage, and loyalty. This lesson, paired with his insatiable passion for everything I.T. and his desire to help others, led him to Lucca IT, where he can combine all these interests and flourish while ensuring that everyone he helps can succeed.

Meet

Tanner Davis

Network Administrator

Tanner Davis, serving as an exceptional Veterinary Technology Specialist at Lucca Veterinary Data Security, brings not only his technical expertise but also a deep personal connection to the animal world, evidenced by his beloved pets – a dog and a cat. His profound love for critters of all kinds adds a layer of empathy and understanding to his work, enhancing the development of veterinary technology solutions. This personal touch ensures that the technologies he develops are not only secure and efficient but also deeply attuned to the needs of animals and their caretakers. Davis’s unique combination of professional skills and personal passion for animal welfare has been instrumental in advancing the standards of veterinary care, making him an invaluable asset to Lucca Veterinary Data Security and the wider veterinary community.

Meet

Matt Kane

Director of Client Services

Matt Kane is the Director of Client Services at Lucca, where he works closely with clients and his team to make sure things run smoothly and problems actually get solved. He has a background in veterinary software and veterinary clinic-focused IT support, so he understands both the technical side and the day-to-day needs of clinics. Matt focuses on clear communication, improving processes, and making sure clients feel supported without the runaround. Outside of work, he’s usually spending time with his family or hanging out with his Belgian Malinois, Oliver.
